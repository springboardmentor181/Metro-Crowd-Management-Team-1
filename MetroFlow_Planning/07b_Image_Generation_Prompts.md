# MetroFlow — Image & Video Generation Prompts

**Companion to Doc 7 (Asset Requirements).**
Ready-to-paste prompts for AI image/video tools (Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly, Ideogram, SDXL, Runway/Pika, etc.). Use even though assets are user-provided — for generating options, mockups, or fallbacks.

**Global style suffix — append to every image prompt:**
> `— deep petrol-teal (#0E4B5A) and signal-orange (#F26C2E) color palette, clean government-enterprise aesthetic, futuristic but professional, soft realistic lighting, Indian metro context, high detail, no text, no watermark, no CCTV/surveillance cameras, no faces in focus`

**Negative prompt (SDXL/where supported):**
> `text, watermark, logo, low-res, blurry, distorted, extra limbs, surveillance camera, cctv, closeup faces, cluttered, oversaturated, cartoonish clutter`

**Aspect/size cheatsheet:** hero 16:9 (1920×1080), cards 16:10 (1600×1000), OG 1.91:1 (1200×630), avatars/icons 1:1, illustrations transparent SVG-style.

## E. Usage Notes
- Generate hero (B1) as **3 options** and pick after color-grading to the palette.
- For icon/illustration **sets**, generate as one prompt/sheet for visual consistency, then split.
- All outputs must be **color-graded to the exact palette** (Doc 4 §2) before use — AI tools drift on color.
- Keep faces out of focus / anonymized (privacy stance + no-CCTV brand promise).
- Deliver final assets per Doc 7 formats & naming (`metroflow_<area>_<name>_<variant>`).
